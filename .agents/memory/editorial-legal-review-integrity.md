---
name: Editorial legal review integrity
description: Attribution and revision-binding rules for human approval of case reports.
---

Shared-password administrators may prepare editorial content, but they must never impersonate or substitute for an attributable, active, legally trained reviewer. A legal approval applies only to the exact content revision that was reviewed; any material edit invalidates that approval and requires a new sign-off before publication.

**Why:** Editorial publication is a legal assurance, not merely an administrative state change. Allowing anonymous password sessions or stale approvals would let materially altered, unsupported content appear human-reviewed.

**How to apply:** Keep preparation permissions separate from review/publication permissions. Bind each approval to the current immutable revision, demote or block content edits after review, and re-run source-rights and pinpoint gates under a lock when publishing.
