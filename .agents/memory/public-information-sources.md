---
name: Public information sources
description: Rules for customer-facing portal counts, subscriber statistics, pricing, and research-status claims.
---

Customer-facing subscriber totals must come only from administrator-published database records and must show their latest update date. Do not use fabricated fallback counts to make an empty community-statistics section look established.

**Why:** Marketing copy must remain truthful when reporting access, adoption, and research availability. Static fallback numbers and conflating product availability with checkout eligibility make this drift silently.

**How to apply:** Treat the public research-status endpoint as the source for corpus pipeline metrics, and label portal-specific curated libraries separately. Keep the portal catalogue explicit about `live` versus `coming soon`; whether a live portal is available through self-service checkout is a separate property. Stripe checkout is the final source for purchasable price and entitlement confirmation.
