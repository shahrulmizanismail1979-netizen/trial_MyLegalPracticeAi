---
name: LAWYes research source policy
description: Governs the separation between verified-library research and public-web research in LAWYes.
---

LAWYes must default to rights-approved, human-verified Malaysian judgment retrieval and draft only from exact returned passages. If that lane has no relevant authority, it fails closed and does not draft.

Public-web research may remain available only as a separate, explicit lawyer-selected lane. It must never be selected automatically, and its sources and output must remain visibly unverified.

**Why:** A silent fallback would make public-web material appear to have the library's verification and rights status. Current processing permission is not the same as subscriber-display permission.

**How to apply:** Any LAWYes research or drafting entry point must carry an explicit source mode through retrieval, drafting instructions, response metadata, saved citations, and the UI.
