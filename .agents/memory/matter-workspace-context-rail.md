---
name: Matter workspace context rail
description: Project-wide visual and interaction preference for the inside of legal-practice apps.
---

Legal matter workspaces should keep the primary work area spacious and direct, with a compact context rail at the upper right containing Progress, Outputs, and Sources. The result should feel simple and efficient without becoming bare or simplistic.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose this restrained pattern as the preferred inside-app workspace across the specialist legal products. It keeps active work central while making status, generated work, and supporting material immediately available.

**How to apply:** Reuse the shared matter workspace treatment where possible. Populate it only from real matter-scoped data, avoid duplicate cards and technical logs, preserve each portal’s branding, and collapse the rail without covering core work on narrow screens.
