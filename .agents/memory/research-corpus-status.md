---
name: Research corpus status
description: How product UI must describe Drive intake and the research publication pipeline without overstating searchable case law.
---

Do not label Google Drive file totals as “cases.” Product-facing corpus status must distinguish catalogued source documents, verified judgments, indexed judgments, and judgments published to customer search.

**Why:** One source file can contain no case or multiple cases, and verification/indexing/publication are separate gates. A large Drive inventory can coexist with no customer-searchable judgments.

**How to apply:** Use the shared corpus-status API/UI for current numbers. Keep any portal-specific static content-library counts clearly separate from the research corpus status.
