## Search 3 — national AI governance
Query: What official Malaysian national AI governance and ethics guidance published in 2024 recommends enterprise accountability, human oversight, privacy, transparency and risk controls?

- **The National Guidelines on AI Governance & Ethics** — Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (MOSTI), September 2024.  
  Official-hosted URL: https://mastic.mosti.gov.my/storage/2024/09/THE-NATIONAL-GUIDELINES-ON-AI-GOVERNANCE-ETHICS.pdf  
  Search evidence: result describes MOSTI publication and its national-guidance status. The document supports internal accountability, risk assessment, transparency, privacy/security, human oversight and documentation; it is guidance, not a general AI statute.
- **The National Guidelines on AI Governance & Ethics** — MOSTI publication mirrored by Universiti Malaysia Sarawak, 23 September 2024.  
  URL: https://cdo.unimas.my/media/attachments/2024/09/23/national-guidelines-of-aige.pdf  
  Search evidence: document title page identifies MOSTI as publisher. Use the MOSTI/MASTIC URL above as the preferred citation.
