# Malaysia governance, risk, data, ESG, cybersecurity and AI — search evidence

## Source 4 — Malaysian Code on Corporate Governance (MCCG), Securities Commission Malaysia
URL: https://www.sc.com.my/api/documentms/download.ashx?id=239e5ea1-a258-4db8-a9e2-41c215bdb776
Date/version: 28 April 2021 (identified in official search result; current baseline surfaced for 2025 research).
Status: Apply-or-explain code for listed issuers, not itself primary legislation.
Search evidence: official result identifies the MCCG as at 28 April 2021. Relevant practitioner baseline: board leadership/effective oversight and disclosure against the Code; board/committee packs need demonstrable consideration of material risks, controls, ethics and sustainability. Do not treat the December 2025 SC discussion paper as enacted.

## Source 5 — Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024, JPDP
URL: https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/akta/personal-data-protection-amendment-act-2024/
Date: 2024 (official statute landing page; enactment/effective provisions must be checked against gazette/commencement orders).
Status: Enacted amendment legislation.
Search evidence: JPDP official result identifies the Act. It is the primary legal source for new/expanded controller and processor obligations; specific commencement phases, thresholds, and transitional timing require verification against the Act and subsidiary instruments.

## Source 6 — National Guidelines on AI Governance & Ethics, MOSTI/MASTIC
URL: https://mastic.mosti.gov.my/storage/2024/09/THE-NATIONAL-GUIDELINES-ON-AI-GOVERNANCE-ETHICS.pdf
Date: September 2024.
Status: Voluntary national guidance, not a generally applicable AI statute.
Search evidence: official PDF search result describes national guidelines published by MOSTI. It supports practical internal governance work—risk assessment, accountable ownership, transparency, privacy/security, human oversight and documentation—but does not by itself create a private-sector licensing or statutory reporting duty.

## Source 7 — Guidelines on Technology Risk Management, Securities Commission Malaysia
URL: https://www.sc.com.my/api/documentms/download.ashx?id=2f253636-07dd-4355-b89e-010b2ef581c1
Date: issued 1 August 2023; Revision 1 dated/effective 19 August 2024.
Status: Regulatory guideline applicable within its capital-market scope; scope must be checked before applying to an ordinary operating company.
Search evidence: official result confirms issuance, revision and effective date. It is relevant to technology/cyber risk accountability for regulated firms, separate from general corporate AI guidance.

## Source 8 — Circular of Personal Data Protection Commissioner No. 1/2025 (Data Breach Notification), JPDP
