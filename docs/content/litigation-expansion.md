# Specialist litigation portal content expansion

**Scope:** Second, content-only enrichment pass for MyLitAI, MyLitAI IRAC, MyCrimAI and MySyariahAI.

## Coverage

| Portal | Enriched tool surfaces | Added guidance |
|---|---|---|
| MyLitAI | Affidavits, Appeals & Jurisdiction, Enforcement & Costs | Document preparation, evidence/record organisation, review checklists, safe input examples and concise FAQs |
| MyLitAI IRAC | Analyzer, Drafting, Reply | Source-set preparation, IRAC quality control, local-precedent handling, reply matrix guidance, instruction examples and FAQs |
| MyCrimAI | Charge Sheet Analyzer, Cross-Examination Helper, Document Drafter | Record preparation, human-review checks, question-sequence and drafting patterns, source-verification cautions and FAQs |
| MySyariahAI | Legal Analyzer, Drafting hub, Client Intake Assistant | Bilingual jurisdiction scoping, State-specific source cautions, local-template review, intake file checks, examples and FAQs |

Each surface now includes multiple practitioner-oriented content types rather than another welcome paragraph: a preparation checklist, a review checklist, a concrete input or workflow pattern, and expandable frequently asked questions.

## Content and source policy

- Guidance is original process material. It does not introduce case names, holdings, filing deadlines, limitation periods or assertions of current substantive law.
- Templates and outputs are expressly described as working aids, not official or court-approved forms.
- Users are directed to verify legislation, rules, forms, authorities, procedural requirements and registry practice from competent current official sources.
- Syariah guidance expressly requires the relevant State or Federal Territory and warns against generalising State-specific sources.
- Unknown facts, dates, sums, citations and procedural entitlements are kept as visible verification items rather than silently completed.
- Existing identifiers, routes, event handlers, authentication, data flows, AI requests/prompts, model defaults and pricing were not changed.
- No backend, database or seed changes were made.

This expansion follows the limits in `docs/content/legal-verification-register.md`: content that would require a fresh legal-source claim was avoided in favour of practitioner preparation and quality-control checklists.
