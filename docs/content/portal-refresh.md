# Specialist portal content refresh coverage

This refresh is limited to customer-facing guidance copy. Routes, handlers, state, authentication, entitlements, prices, payment/provider behavior, backend services and database structures were not changed.

| `mycrimai` | Workspace dashboard introduction and AI-tools guidance | Charge/record inputs; issue-map, checklist and draft outputs; defence/prosecution context and criminal-court review |

## Intentionally unmodified boundaries

- No case names, citations, holdings, ratios, statutory text, section references, court rules, practice directions, fatwa, Quranic text, classical kitab content or other legal corpus material was rewritten.
- No claim was added that an authority, source, AI answer or generated draft is verified, current, binding, complete or filing-ready.
- Existing library counts, subscriber statistics, research-status claims, prices and checkout/entitlement wording were not changed.
- Legal-material data files, sample authorities, templates and substantive reference modules remain outside this refresh and are reserved for the separate legal-material review.
