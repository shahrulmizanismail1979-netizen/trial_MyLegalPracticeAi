# PART 0

# THE INTELLIGENT FRONT DOOR

# PERSONA-FIRST ONBOARDING, ROLE-BASED UI, WORKFLOW & AI CUSTOMISATION

BEFORE THE USER SEES THE MAIN APPLICATION DASHBOARD, THE APPLICATION MUST FIRST UNDERSTAND:

# “WHAT KIND OF LEGAL PROFESSIONAL ARE YOU?”

This must become the FIRST meaningful interaction on the application landing page.

Do NOT immediately throw every user into the same generic dashboard.

Do NOT expose every module to everyone.

Do NOT assume that all legal professionals work in the same way.

The system must first identify the user's professional context and then configure the entire experience accordingly.

---

# THE LANDING PAGE MUST BEGIN WITH A PROFESSIONAL ROLE SELECTION

The first page should be elegant, professional, very simple and immediately understandable.

Display a short introduction such as:

# Welcome.

## Let us set up your legal workspace around the way you actually work.

Then ask:

# HOW DO YOU PRIMARILY WORK IN LAW?

Present THREE clear pathways:

---

# OPTION 1

# LEGAL PRACTITIONER

### I practise law in a law firm or legal practice.

Examples:

* Advocate & Solicitor
* Partner
* Managing Partner
* Sole Practitioner
* Associate
* Legal Assistant
* Syariah Practitioner
* Legal Practice Staff

Primary environment:

# CLIENTS + MATTERS + CASE FILES + LAW FIRM MANAGEMENT

---

# OPTION 2

# IN-HOUSE COUNSEL

### I manage legal work inside a company, organisation, institution or group.

Examples:

* General Counsel
* Head of Legal
* Legal Counsel
* Corporate Counsel
* Compliance Counsel
* Legal Manager
* Company Legal Department

Primary environment:

# INTERNAL LEGAL MATTERS + CONTRACTS + ADVISORY + COMPLIANCE + BUSINESS RISK + EXTERNAL COUNSEL MANAGEMENT

---

# OPTION 3

# LAW LECTURER / LEGAL ACADEMIC

### I teach, research or work academically in law.

Examples:

* Law Lecturer
* Professor
* Legal Researcher
* Postgraduate Supervisor
* Academic Administrator
* Research Centre Member

Primary environment:

# TEACHING + RESEARCH + WRITING + SUPERVISION + LEGAL KNOWLEDGE + ACADEMIC MANAGEMENT

---

# THE APPLICATION SHOULD HAVE ONE CORE ENGINE BUT THREE PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCES

Do NOT build three completely unrelated applications.

Use:

# ONE SHARED LEGAL INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM

with:

# THREE ROLE-SPECIFIC WORKSPACES.

Architect shared capabilities intelligently.

Examples of shared capabilities:

* authentication;
* AI engine;
* document intelligence;
* legal research;
* case law;
* legislation;
* search;
* file management;
* drafting engine;
* knowledge base;
* calendar;
* tasks;
* notifications;
* user settings;
* secure storage.

Then configure different workflows around them.

---

# ROLE SELECTION MUST BE SAVED

Once the user chooses a professional role:

save it to the user profile.

On future logins:

load the appropriate workspace automatically.

Do NOT repeatedly ask the same question.

However, provide:

# SWITCH PROFESSIONAL MODE

inside Settings or Profile.

This is important because some users may simultaneously be:

* practitioner + lecturer;
* in-house counsel + lecturer;
* consultant + academic;
* practitioner + corporate adviser.

Allow additional professional modes to be enabled later without requiring a second account.

---

# PRIMARY ROLE AND SECONDARY ROLES

Support:

PRIMARY PROFESSIONAL ROLE

and optionally:

SECONDARY PROFESSIONAL ROLE(S).

The PRIMARY role controls:

* default dashboard;
* home screen;
* navigation priority;
* default terminology.

Secondary roles can unlock relevant additional modules.

Do NOT clutter the initial onboarding with excessive questions.

Ask the primary role first.

Then progressively personalise.
