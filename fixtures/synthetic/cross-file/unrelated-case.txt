CHARGE

1. The accused was charged under Section 39B(1)(a) of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952
for trafficking in a controlled substance. The charge reads:

   "That you, on 10 March 2022 at approximately 14:30 hours, at Jalan Padungan,
   Kuching, Sarawak, did traffic in a dangerous drug, namely methamphetamine, with
   a total nett weight of 243.7 grammes, and you have thereby committed an offence
   under section 39B(1)(a) of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952 and punishable under
   section 39B(2) of the same Act."

PROSECUTION CASE

2. The prosecution called eight witnesses. The arresting officer, PW1, testified that
he observed the accused loitering near a parked vehicle. Upon a lawful search, a
plastic bag containing white crystalline substance was found.

3. The chemist, PW3 (Dr. Leong Swee Lin), confirmed the substance tested positive
for methamphetamine with a nett weight of 243.7 grammes.

4. PW6 (Customs Officer Roslan bin Hamid) confirmed the chain of custody of the
exhibit from seizure to analysis.

DEFENCE

5. The accused elected to give evidence on oath. He testified that he had no
knowledge of the drugs. He claimed the plastic bag had been placed in his car
by an unknown person while he was at a nearby coffee shop.

6. The accused called two witnesses who confirmed he was at the coffee shop but
neither could account for his vehicle being unattended.

FINDING

7. This Court finds that the prosecution has proven its case beyond a reasonable
doubt. The accused's defence of ignorance is rejected as an afterthought and
inherently improbable.

8. The accused is convicted of the charge.

SENTENCE

9. Pursuant to Section 39B(2) of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952, the mandatory
sentence of death is imposed.
