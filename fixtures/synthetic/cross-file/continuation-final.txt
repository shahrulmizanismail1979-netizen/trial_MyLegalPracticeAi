Further consideration of the evidence:

38. Having reviewed the documentary evidence in totality, this Court accepts the
Plaintiff's version of events. The contemporaneous emails exchanged between the
parties in February 2021 are particularly persuasive.

39. The Defendant's reliance on the force majeure clause fails. The clause requires
notice within 7 days of the triggering event. No such notice was given.

40. The Defendant's expert witness, Mr. David Lim, conceded under cross-examination
that an industry-standard supplier would have had contingency stock to meet the
contractual delivery date notwithstanding supply-chain disruptions of the magnitude
described.

DAMAGES

41. The Plaintiff has proven damages of RM 320,000 for lost profits on forward
contracts that could not be fulfilled due to the Defendant's breach.

42. The Plaintiff is also entitled to consequential damages of RM 45,000 for the
additional procurement costs incurred in sourcing substitute components on short
notice.

43. Pre-judgment interest at 5% per annum from the date of breach (1 March 2021) to
the date of this judgment.

CONCLUSION

44. This Court accordingly enters judgment for the Plaintiff in the total sum of RM
365,000 plus interest as stated above.

45. Costs are awarded to the Plaintiff, to be taxed if not agreed.

Order accordingly.
