GROUNDS OF JUDGMENT

[1] This is a construction dispute arising out of a contract for the construction of a commercial complex in Kuala Lumpur.

[2] The Plaintiff is a construction company that was engaged by the Defendant to construct a 30-storey commercial complex at Jalan Ampang, Kuala Lumpur.

[3] The contract was valued at RM 45,000,000.00 (Ringgit Malaysia Forty-Five Million) and was executed on 15 March 2020.

[4] The Plaintiff claims that the Defendant failed to make progress payments as agreed under the contract, resulting in the Plaintiff's cash flow difficulties.

[5] The Defendant counterclaims that the Plaintiff failed to complete the works within the contractual period and the quality of the works was defective.

BACKGROUND FACTS

[6] The parties entered into a standard form construction contract based on the PAM 2006 (With Quantities) form.

[7] Having considered the evidence, judgment is entered for the Plaintiff in the sum of RM 7,320,000.00.
