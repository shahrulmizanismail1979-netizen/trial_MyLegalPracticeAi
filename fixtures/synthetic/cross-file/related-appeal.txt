INTRODUCTION

1. This is an appeal by the Defendant (now Appellant) against the High Court's
decision dated 12 May 2023 in Suit No. 22NCVC-123-06/2023 in which the High Court
granted judgment to the Respondent (Plaintiff below) for RM 166,000 net.

2. The central issue on appeal is whether the High Court correctly applied the
doctrine of substantial performance in awarding full contract price for partial
delivery.

GROUNDS OF APPEAL

3. The Appellant submits that:

   (a) The doctrine of substantial performance does not apply where less than 65%
       of goods were delivered;
   (b) The learned Judicial Commissioner erred in awarding cost of all 320 units
       when only 280 units met the contractual specification;
   (c) The damages for the shortfall were underestimated.

DECISION

4. We have carefully considered the grounds of appeal and the High Court's reasoning.

5. On the first issue, this Court affirms the Judicial Commissioner's application
of the doctrine of substantial performance. The delivery of 320 out of 500 units
(64%) falls within the zone of substantial performance given the nature of the goods
and the commercial relationship.

6. On the second issue, the Appellant's argument that only 280 units met
specification was not pleaded below and is not permitted to be raised on appeal for
the first time: see _Lembaga Kemajuan Tanah Persekutuan v Tenaga Nasional Bhd_ [2010]
3 MLJ 449.

7. The appeal is accordingly dismissed with costs of RM 15,000 here and below.

ORDER

The appeal is dismissed.

Costs of RM 15,000 to be paid by the Appellant to the Respondent.
