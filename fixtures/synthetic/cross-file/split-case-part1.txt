GROUNDS OF JUDGMENT

[1] This is a construction dispute arising out of a contract for the construction of a commercial complex in Kuala Lumpur.

[2] The Plaintiff is a construction company that was engaged by the Defendant to construct a 30-storey commercial complex at Jalan Ampang, Kuala Lumpur.

[3] The contract was valued at RM 45,000,000.00 (Ringgit Malaysia Forty-Five Million) and was executed on 15 March 2020.

[4] The Plaintiff claims that the Defendant failed to make progress payments as agreed under the contract, resulting in the Plaintiff's cash flow difficulties.

[5] The Defendant counterclaims that the Plaintiff failed to complete the works within the contractual period and the quality of the works was defective.

[6] The issues before the Court are:
(a) Whether the Defendant was in breach of contract by failing to make progress payments;
(b) Whether the Plaintiff was in delay in completing the works;
(c) Whether there were defects in the Plaintiff's works; and
(d) The appropriate reliefs to be granted.

BACKGROUND FACTS

[7] The parties entered into a standard form construction contract based on the PAM 2006 (With Quantities) form.

[8] The construction works were scheduled to be completed by 15 September 2022 (30 months from commencement).

[9] Progress Claim No. 1 to Progress Claim No. 8 were submitted by the Plaintiff between April 2020 and November 2021 totalling RM 22,500,000.00.

[10] The Defendant only paid RM 15,000,000.00, leaving an outstanding balance of RM 7,500,000.00.

[11] The Plaintiff issued a Notice of Delay to the Defendant on 5 December 2021 citing lack of payment as the cause of delay.

[12] The Defendant issued a Notice of Defects on 10 January 2022 identifying 127 alleged defects in the works.

[13] The Plaintiff disputes the validity of the defects notice, contending that the alleged defects were either non-existent or minor in nature.
