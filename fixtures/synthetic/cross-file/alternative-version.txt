INTRODUCTION

[1]  This action concerns a construction contract dispute in which the Plaintiff, a
construction company, claims against the Defendant for unpaid progress payments
under a contract for the construction of a 30-storey commercial complex at Jalan
Ampang, Kuala Lumpur.  The Defendant counterclaims for delay and defects.

[2]  This is an alternative editorial version of the same judgment ([2022] MLJU 2501).
The citation, parties, court, and ratio decidendi are identical to the primary version
but the editorial headings, paragraph arrangement, and subheadings differ in
accordance with this publisher's house style.

BACKGROUND FACTS

[3]  The contract between the parties was dated 15 March 2020 and valued at
RM 45,000,000.00.  It was in the standard PAM 2006 (With Quantities) form.

[4]  The Plaintiff submitted Progress Claims No. 1 through No. 8 between April 2020
and November 2021, totalling RM 22,500,000.00.  The Defendant paid only
RM 15,000,000.00, leaving an unpaid balance of RM 7,500,000.00.

[5]  On 5 December 2021 the Plaintiff issued a Notice of Delay attributing the delay
to non-payment.  On 10 January 2022 the Defendant responded with a Notice of
Defects listing 127 alleged items.

FINDINGS OF THE COURT

[6]  The Court finds the Defendant in breach of the progress payment obligations.
No credible basis was advanced to withhold the certified sums.

[7]  On delay: the Court finds the proximate cause to be the Defendant's failure to
honour certified progress claims.  The Notice of Delay was validly issued.

[8]  On defects: the Court is not satisfied that the 127 alleged defects were
substantiated.  The Plaintiff's expert evidence is preferred.  Most items are
either non-existent or of a de minimis character.

[9]  Judgment is entered for the Plaintiff in the sum of RM 7,320,000.00.

[10]  The Defendant's counterclaim is dismissed with costs.

[11]  Costs of the action to the Plaintiff, to be taxed if not agreed.

Order accordingly.
