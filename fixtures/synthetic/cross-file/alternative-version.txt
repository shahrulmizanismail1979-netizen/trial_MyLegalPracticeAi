BACKGROUND

2. The Plaintiff claims damages for breach of a supply contract. The Defendant
denies liability and counterclaims for losses arising from the Plaintiff's alleged
failure to deliver goods on time.

3. The facts are largely undisputed. Parties entered into a Supply Agreement dated
1 January 2022. The Plaintiff was to deliver 500 units of industrial compressors by
30 June 2022. Only 320 units were delivered by that date.

FINDINGS

4. This Court finds, on a balance of probabilities, that the Plaintiff was in breach
of the Supply Agreement by failing to deliver the full quantity by the stipulated date.

5. The Defendant's counterclaim succeeds in part. Damages for the shortfall of
180 units are assessed at RM 90,000 (being RM 500 per unit).

6. The Plaintiff's claim for the price of goods delivered (320 units × RM 800 = RM
256,000) succeeds. The Defendant is entitled to set off the counterclaim award.

CONCLUSION AND ORDERS

7. Accordingly, this Court makes the following orders:

   (a) Judgment for the Plaintiff in the sum of RM 256,000;
   (b) Set-off of the Defendant's counterclaim of RM 90,000;
   (c) Net judgment sum: RM 166,000 in favour of the Plaintiff;
   (d) Interest at 5% per annum from the date of filing until full settlement;
   (e) Costs to the Plaintiff, to be taxed if not agreed.

Order accordingly.
