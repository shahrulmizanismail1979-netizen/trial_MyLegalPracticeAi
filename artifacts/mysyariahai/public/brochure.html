Legal Provisions
Peruntukan Undang-Undang
How to use:
Type any keyword in the search bar or filter by category (e.g., Marriage, Divorce, Property). Click any provision to view its full text, section reference, and related commentary. Then hit "Cross-Reference with AI" to find every case, fatwa, and verse connected to it.
Case Laws
Kes Undang-Undang
How to use:
Browse 86 reported Shariah court cases. Each case is structured into 4 tabs: Facts, Issues, Held, and Significance -- so you can jump straight to the part you need. Search by keyword or filter by category. One click sends any case to the AI Analyzer for full cross-referencing.
Quranic Verses
Ayat Al-Quran
How to use:
40 key Quranic verses with full Arabic text, English and Malay translations, and a detailed explanation of their legal relevance. Filter by categories like Family Law, Criminal Law, Property, Evidence, and more. Every verse links directly to the AI Analyzer.
Gazetted Fatwas
Fatwa Bergazet
How to use:
15 officially gazetted Malaysian fatwas with summary and detailed views. See the issuing authority, gazette reference number, full reasoning, and legal implications. Filter by category. Use "Cross-Reference with AI" to find supporting provisions and cases.
Legislation Reference
Rujukan Perundangan
How to use:
Quick-access directory of Malaysian Islamic law statutes and enactments. Covers both federal-level and state-level Shariah legislation including Act 303 and state enactments. Search by keyword to find the right statute instantly.
Legal Glossary
Glosari Undang-Undang
How to use:
Comprehensive trilingual glossary of Arabic, English, and Malay legal terms. Browse alphabetically using the letter filter or type any term in the search bar. Each entry includes a full definition and context in Shariah legal practice.
